# Public copy and working documents

Joshua asked on September 17 to remove excessive operational language from the public site, citing the opening disclaimer on the operating explanation. This editorial revision leads with members' interests, explains the practical rules in ordinary language, and gives readers a direct action. A short proposal-for-2027 status remains clear.

This is a presentation change to the September 16 proposal. It does not adopt new coalition policy, announce a sponsor or introduced bill, establish present legal duties, resolve enforceability, or supply a fiscal estimate. It does not amend the external petition or authorize correspondence.

The [operational working paper](2026-09-16-operational-crosswalk.md) retains the detailed legal crosswalk, drafting definitions, manager obligations, signatory questions, contract transition, compliance authority and cost worksheet. Those questions belong in the review packet rather than repeated at the start of public pages. The legislator page keeps one concise explanation that legal and fiscal review will shape the final bill.

## Preserved substance

- Prospective, issuer-neutral pension coverage; Treasury remains separate.
- Board-approved manager limits and a financial record for every covered purchase.
- Shared core research for controlled pools with each system's allocation, financial finding and approval.
- Review of commitments to genuinely external funds; reporting rights and dated known exposure; underlying-investment treatment for controlled sleeves and targeted vehicles.
- Qualified preparers, conflicts disclosure and review independent of issuer/seller for initial mandates, new issuers and material changes.
- Analysis before a binding commitment and publication within 30 calendar days, with settlement and cancellation status identified separately.
- Different treatment of late publication and missing prior analysis, contractual duties and audit oversight, without automatic liquidation for a clerical delay.
- Existing financial evidence, source locators and historical downloads preserved. No zero-cost or filing-ready claim.

The public homepage, proposal, examples, legislative brief, action letter, about page and current downloads should explain what the reform would mean to members. Dated corrections and source pages still carry necessary evidence limits. Preserve old heading links when simplifying headings, and label earlier dated handouts as historical.
