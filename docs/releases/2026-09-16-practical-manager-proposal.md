# Practical manager and pooled-fund proposal

## Authorization and scope

On September 16, Joshua explicitly requested that the researched recommendations be implemented locally, pushed to GitHub and published. This release carries out that instruction after content review and validation. It does not record a separate user review of a source fingerprint.

The campaign proposal now specifies:

- Delegated trading inside board-approved mandates, with a financial record for each covered purchase.
- Shared research for controlled pools, with separate plan allocations, findings and responsible officials.
- Commitment-level review for genuinely external funds, with periodic known-exposure reporting and an anti-avoidance rule for controlled sleeves and targeted vehicles.
- Qualified preparers, named approval, conflicts disclosure and review independent of issuer/seller for initial mandates, new issuers and material changes.
- Analysis before the binding commitment; publication within 30 calendar days of that commitment, identifying settlement status separately.
- Pension-system publication responsibility, differentiated treatment of late records and missing pre-purchase analysis, and proposed oversight subject to legal drafting.
- Legal, contract-transition and cost review still pending. No introduced bill, sponsor, legal engagement, official fiscal estimate or coalition adoption of these detailed rules is claimed.

The release adds `/the-act/how-it-works/`, aligns the Act, legislator, audience and action pages, updates the working paper, and publishes a September 16 PDF and letter. September 15 files are preserved byte for byte and labeled historical. No source exhibit or financial transaction figure changes.
