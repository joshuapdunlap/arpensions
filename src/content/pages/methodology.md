Good advocacy depends on source-faithful claims and a public record readers can check.

## We request the decision records

The investigation collects agency productions covering packets, minutes, correspondence, contracts, analysis, transactions and holdings. Original evidence is preserved separately from published derivatives. A source record identifies its agency, date, original filename and locator.

## We read the file in context

Text extraction and automated search are locator aids. Rendered pages, original spreadsheet rows and surrounding material matter when layout or data types affect meaning. We do not treat a failed keyword search as proof that a record does not exist.

## We tie important claims to a specific record

Signed minutes establish the motion recorded. A schedule establishes a planned meeting. A seller's message establishes the seller's account. A completed bank record carries more weight for completion than an earlier notice. Each source page explains what it supports and its limits.

## We organize the money by decision stage

Authorization, funding, purchase, settlement, holding, maturity and processing are distinct. Par and value including accrued income are also distinct. A later holding of the same security is not an additional purchase.

The $84.9 million direct-security floor combines different record dates. The $125 million mixed-stage measure substitutes ATRS funding for the ATRS security inside it. Treasury's processing payment is excluded. [See the background measures, components and sources](/evidence/treasury/#cross-agency-measures).

## We bound absence findings

The reviewed APERS productions do not identify an independent consultant sovereign-credit memo tied to the purchases. That does not exclude oral or unproduced work. Aon supplied substantive implementation advice; Treasury supplied internal credit analysis; ATRS possessed external rating material. [Read the analysis finding](/findings/procedural-asymmetry/).

## We protect private and operational information

Published derivatives exclude private and operational identifiers where required. A derivative must preserve the source's substantive words, amounts, dates and status. Evidence images are literal source pages or rectangular crops, with explanatory captions outside the image.

## We correct the public account

New evidence can resolve earlier gaps. [Corrections](/corrections/) explains the September changes. Historical dated downloads remain historical; the current explanation is this site's reviewed September content. No archive-wide record-count headline is used without a new count audit.
