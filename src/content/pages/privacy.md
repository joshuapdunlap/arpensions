The research site does not use an analytics tracker or maintain a supporter database.

## Site visits

The site serves its own fonts and styles. GitHub Pages infrastructure may process ordinary network request information. External links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

## Petition submissions

Action Network hosts and processes the external petition. A submission provides the information entered to that service and the organization administering the petition. Review Action Network's subscription terms and preferences before signing.

The campaign uses information provided for the requested action and campaign administration, with communications governed by the form's actual settings and your choices. [Action Network privacy policy](https://actionnetwork.org/privacy).

## Email and source questions

Email to [info@arpensions.org](mailto:info@arpensions.org) contains the information you choose to include. The campaign uses it to respond, maintain correspondence or complete your requested action. Do not send pension account numbers, government identification or other sensitive personal records.

## Your choices

For questions, updates or deletion requests concerning campaign-held information, email [info@arpensions.org](mailto:info@arpensions.org?subject=Privacy%20request) with the subject Privacy request. Include enough context to identify the submission without sending sensitive identification.
