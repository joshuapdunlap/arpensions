A source and drafting briefing for legislators considering the issuer-neutral pension proposal.

## Status and request

**Public money deserves a public investment record.** The Pension Investment Integrity Act is a campaign proposal for the 2027 Arkansas regular session. This brief announces no bill number or legislative sponsor. It does not represent enacted law or a filed bill.

According to Arkansans for Pension Integrity's June 27, 2026 convention record, Citizens First Congress delegates selected pension investment integrity for the coalition's short-term 2027 priority platform. That is campaign-reported coalition selection, not legislative adoption.

Consider sponsorship and request a source and drafting briefing. [Download the one-page handout (PDF)](/assets/documents/pension-investment-integrity-act-brief-2026-09-15.pdf) · [Read the accessible web brief](/legislators/one-page/).

## The proposed decision record

The proposal calls for pre-acquisition credit analysis, an alternatives comparison, a liquidity explanation and a written financial determination, followed by publication of the core record within 30 days of a covered acquisition. [Read the five safeguards and intended coverage](/the-act/).

## Why the records make the case

APERS reported $25 million in two positions on September 7. ATRS's $50 million manager funding completed January 2, and the account bought a $9.9 million Israel Bond on February 17. Members should be able to connect those commitments to the financial work underlying the decisions. [APERS custody, p. 1](/documents/records/apers-september-holdings/) · [ATRS funding, p. 3](/documents/records/atrs-completed-funding/) · [ATRS trade, row 2](/documents/records/atrs-completed-purchase/).

Aon supplied substantive manager-selection and implementation advice. The reform asks for an identifiable decision-specific credit, alternatives and liquidity case alongside that advice. [Aon memo, pp. 149–150](/documents/records/atrs-aon-memo/).

## Scope and design

The proposal is prospective and issuer-neutral. Staff or an independent adviser could prepare the financial work; trustees would be responsible for the written fiduciary finding and investment judgment. The intended coverage is Arkansas pension benefit plans, including ATRS, APERS, ASHERS, ASPRS, AJRS and LOPFI. Trustees would retain the final buy, hold or sell decision. The separately governed State Treasury is a comparison, not a covered pension plan under this proposal.

Bill drafting must resolve **manager coverage**: when a separate-account manager acquires a covered security, who prepares the analysis, who signs the determination and what triggers public posting? An authorization or transfer to a manager should not be mistaken for the underlying security acquisition. Pooled investment vehicles also require a clear, workable coverage definition. These are drafting questions, not settled statutory text.

Publication would follow acquisition, within 30 days. Narrow, explained redactions may protect legally confidential account, operational or proprietary details. The public should still receive the amount, acquisition date, credit conclusion, material risks, alternatives and liquidity comparison, and financial determination.

## Decisions for the drafting table

These scenarios identify unresolved drafting choices, not settled statutory text.

| Scenario | Present direction | Questions to resolve |
|---|---|---|
| Direct pension acquisition | Financial work before acquisition; trustees retain investment authority; core record posted within 30 days afterward. | Define covered instruments, the acquisition event, preparer qualifications and signatory responsibilities. |
| Separate-account manager purchase | Address the underlying purchase separately from authorization or funding. | Who prepares and approves the record, and how does the manager notify the system in time for publication? |
| Pooled vehicle | Coverage requires a workable definition. | Does the rule cover subscriptions, underlying trades or both? What information can the system obtain and publish? |
| Renewal or rollover | Treatment remains unresolved. | When is renewed exposure a new acquisition, and when must analysis be refreshed? |
| Add-on purchase | Treatment remains unresolved. | When is prior analysis adequate, who records material changes, and does each purchase start a posting deadline? |

Drafting must also specify retention, compliance responsibility, correction of missed deadlines, transition rules and administrative costs. Enforcement and remedies are not settled. A statute-by-statute crosswalk and qualified Arkansas legislative/legal review are still needed. This brief makes no claim that the proposed safeguards are already required by current law.

The concern about absent independent recommendations should be distinguished from the proposed rule: the present proposal permits staff-prepared analysis. Qualifications and any independence requirements need explicit drafting decisions.

## Legislative calendar

The official calendar lists **November 16, 2026** for prefiling, **January 11, 2027** for convening and **January 25, 2027** as the ordinary retirement-legislation filing deadline. The calendar notes possible deadline extension if both chambers recess more than four days and a three-fourths vote of each chamber's full membership for later introduction. [Bureau of Legislative Research calendar, pp. 1–3](https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Home/FTPDocument?path=%2Fassembly%2F2027%2FDocuments%2FSession_Important_Dates_2027.pdf), verified September 16, 2026.

## Legislative ask

Request a source and drafting briefing, help resolve manager coverage, and consider sponsoring the proposal for the 2027 regular session. Ask for a consistent public procedure while preserving trustees' responsibility for investment decisions.
