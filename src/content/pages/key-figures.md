These roles describe the people and institutions in the dated records. They are not a live directory of current officeholders.

## Public officials and board representatives

**Dennis Milligan and Jason Brady.** The records concern Milligan's Auditor of State office. APERS minutes identify Brady as the Auditor's proxy and the presenter and mover of the May 15, 2025 authorization. ATRS records identify him as the Auditor's designee. [APERS minutes, pp. 1 and 4](/documents/records/apers-authorization/) · [ATRS designee record](/documents/records/atrs-auditor-designee/).

**Jim Hudson and Andy Babbitt.** Babbitt's April 16, 2025 DFA introduction describes their meeting with the seller representatives the previous day. It identifies Babbitt as DFA Deputy Director and State Controller. The message confirms the encounter without resolving every itinerary detail. [Participant message](/documents/records/hudson-babbitt-confirmation/).

## Pension systems and advisers

**Aon.** ATRS's consultant compared implementation options, recommended Reams and noted limited marketability. Its disclaimer says the memo is not a recommendation to invest or not invest in Israel Bonds and disclaims recommendations on individual securities. [Memo, pp. 149–150](/documents/records/atrs-aon-memo/).

**Reams Asset Management.** The ATRS manager account received $50 million on January 2, 2026. It bought a $9.9 million Israel Bond in February and reported that position alongside U.S. Treasuries and cash in July. [ATRS dossier](/evidence/atrs/).

**Mark White and Rod Graves.** White appears on the April outreach itinerary in the ATRS Executive Director role. Graves sent the December 29 funding notice in his ATRS role. Scheduling alone does not prove a White meeting, and the December notice is superseded as completion evidence by the January bank record. [Itinerary](/documents/records/capitol-itinerary/) · [Notice](/documents/records/atrs-manager-funding/).

**Amy Fecher and Carlos Borromeo.** Seller correspondence reports an encounter with Fecher in the APERS Executive Director role. Borromeo's February 2026 chief investment officer email addresses the search for Board analysis. [Seller account](/documents/records/seller-reported-meetings/) · [Search email](/documents/records/apers-analysis-email/).

## Seller and outreach

**Development Corporation for Israel, Lawrence Berman and Brad Young.** The seller representatives appear in the itinerary, introductions and follow-up. Seller accounts establish what the seller reported; completed agency records establish institutional actions. [Outreach record](/documents/records/ashers-route/) · [Regulatory and liquidity finding](/findings/regulatory-record/).

**State Financial Officers Foundation.** Agency records show sponsor listings, circulation of another state's purchase, speech preparation and an offer to connect member-state contacts. [Network finding](/findings/sfof-pipeline/).

No individual is described here as the lone dissenter. Procedural concern and a recorded individual vote are different claims.
