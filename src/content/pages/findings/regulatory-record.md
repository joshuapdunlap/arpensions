Historical regulatory records and limited marketability belong in due diligence, with their outcomes intact.

## The sales channel

Aon's memorandum identifies limited marketability. A decision record should explain transfer restrictions, execution arrangements, costs and alternatives, including what return compensates the fund for the liquidity it gives up. [Aon memo, pp. 149–150](/documents/records/atrs-aon-memo/).

## The regulatory history

Arkansas's April 7, 1986 order suspended Development Corporation for Israel's registration for a principal-designation deficiency. The July 22 order vacated that suspension and reinstated registration after documentation was supplied. These orders contain no fraud or investor-harm finding. Leaving out the reinstatement would misrepresent the history. [Orders 86-27-S and 86-27a-S](/documents/records/securities-1986-orders/).

FINRA's public report identifies the firm as CRD 11148 and provides regulatory disclosures. Read each matter's allegations and disposition together; historical disclosure is not proof that the Arkansas pension transactions were unlawful. [Official BrokerCheck report](https://files.brokercheck.finra.org/firm/firm_11148.pdf), reviewed September 15, 2026.

## The financial review question

The public case for a covered purchase should address terms, risks, available alternatives and execution costs. This site makes no legal finding about a violation from the sales structure or regulatory history alone.
