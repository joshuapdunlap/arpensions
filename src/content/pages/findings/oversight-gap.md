The September production answers a question this site previously left open: what followed the ATRS manager-account transfer.

## Funding is now verified

A December 29 notice described funding. The completed bank record dates the $50 million transfer to **January 2, 2026**. [Completed funding, p. 3](/documents/records/atrs-completed-funding/).

## The account bought a mix of assets

The completed Israel Bond purchase was **$9.9 million on February 17**. The first issuance was canceled and replaced. July reporting shows the same security alongside U.S. Treasuries and cash, with a total account value of $50,446,757.95 including accrued income. [Trade row 2](/documents/records/atrs-completed-purchase/) · [Cancellation](/documents/records/atrs-cancellation/) · [July rows 7, 9 and 12](/documents/records/atrs-july-holdings/).

The $50 million funding is not a $50 million Israel Bonds holding. Adding the $9.9 million bond to the full funding would double count it.

## What remains open

The records now provide security-level transactions, holdings and manager reporting. The remaining question concerns the decision-specific merits analysis behind the sovereign exposure. Manager implementation reporting and independent credit advice have different purposes. [Aon's scope, pp. 149–150](/documents/records/atrs-aon-memo/) · [Q2 manager reporting](/documents/records/atrs-q2-report/).
